## Krastorio 2 loaders

The native loader definitions and loader-graphics.lua are adapted from
Krastorio 2 by Linver, Krastor and raiguard, licensed GNU LGPL version 3.
Source commit: 7e17e7b977a7f96fac1bcff99a30d06f568dda7a
https://github.com/raiguard/Krastorio2/tree/7e17e7b977a7f96fac1bcff99a30d06f568dda7a/prototypes/buildings

Loader PNGs are from Krastorio2Assets, licensed GNU LGPL version 3.
Source commit: 8327c886c611b825b9c76e9ab0a574589298146b
https://github.com/raiguard/Krastorio2Assets/tree/8327c886c611b825b9c76e9ab0a574589298146b

Files: buildings/loader/kr-loader{,-mask,-rust}.png and
icons/entities/{loader,fast-loader,express-loader,advanced-loader}.png.
These PNGs are unmodified. The advanced-loader icon represents the turbo tier.
Changes to code: local asset paths, Factorio Light names, four vanilla belt
tiers, vanilla ingredients, splitter research unlocks, stacking, insertion
limits, and fixed legendary health. The original snapping script is not used.
No Krastorio dependency or other Krastorio content is included.
