Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-11-03
  Features:
    - Fixed legendary benefits for official Space Age machinery, logistics, combat and equipment, with Quality disabled.
    - Removed spoilage timers and the spoilage item; yumako mash supplies remaining biomass recipes.
    - Hand-craftable mash nutrients, bacteria incubation and productivity-safe iron/copper isolation.
    - All eight ordinary/logistic chest types can be placed on platforms.
    - Temperature-preserving filling and emptying for all 22 real vanilla fluids.
    - Four native compact loaders unlocked with the matching belt/splitter technology.
    - Removed the pistol; freeplay starts and respawns use a submachine gun and 100 yellow magazines.
  Compatibility:
    - Requires Factorio 2.1.24+ in the 2.1 series, Space Age, Elevated Rails and Recycler.
    - Quality is incompatible. Intended for new freeplay games.
    - Planet-specific recipe restrictions and platform cargo-bay access remain unchanged.
